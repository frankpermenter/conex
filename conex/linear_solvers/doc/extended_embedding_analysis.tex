\section{Setup}

Consider the extended embedding of a conic program with cone $\mathcal{C} = \R^n_+$:
\begin{align}
  \min \quad & \alpha \theta \notag \\
  \text{s.t.} \quad & Ax - b\tau = r_p \theta \label{eq:primal} \\
  & -A^T y - s + c\tau = r_d \theta \label{eq:dual} \\
  & \ip{b}{y} - \ip{c}{x} - \kappa = r_g \theta \label{eq:gap} \\
  & \ip{r_p}{y} + \ip{r_d}{x} + r_g \tau = -\alpha \label{eq:norm} \\
  & x \in \R^n_+, \; s \in \R^n_+, \; \tau \geq 0, \; \kappa \geq 0 \notag
\end{align}
where $A \in \R^{m \times n}$, $b \in \R^m$, $c \in \R^n$, and $y \in \R^m$, $\theta \in \R$ are free variables. The parameters $r_p, r_d, r_g, \alpha$ are residuals at a fixed strictly feasible point $(\hat{x}, \hat{s}, \hat{y}, \hat{\tau}, \hat{\kappa})$.

\section{Cone variable and scaling}

The cone variables are $(x, s, \tau, \kappa) \in \R^n_+ \times \R^n_+ \times \R_+ \times \R_+$. This is a product of $2n + 2$ nonneg scalar cones with rank $R = 2n + 2$.

Let $W = \diag(w_x, w_s, w_\tau, w_\kappa)$ be the scaling matrix, where $w_x \in \R^n_{++}$, $w_s \in \R^n_{++}$, $w_\tau > 0$, $w_\kappa > 0$. For the nonneg cone, the quadratic representation is $P(W^{1/2}) = \diag(W)$ (componentwise multiplication).

\section{Partitioned Newton direction}

Partition the direction as $d = (d_x, d_s, d_\tau, d_\kappa)$ with $d_x, d_s \in \R^n$ and $d_\tau, d_\kappa \in \R$.

\subsection{Defining linear system}

The primal variables of the KKT system are
\[
  z = (x, y, s, \tau, \kappa, \theta) \in \R^{2n + m + 3}.
\]
The cone constraints $x \geq 0$, $s \geq 0$, $\tau \geq 0$, $\kappa \geq 0$ contribute the Gram matrix
\[
  G = \diag(\underbrace{w_x^2}_{n}, \underbrace{0}_{m}, \underbrace{w_s^2}_{n}, w_\tau^2, w_\kappa^2, 0).
\]
Note: $G$ has zeros at the free variable positions ($y$ and $\theta$).

The four equality constraints \eqref{eq:primal}--\eqref{eq:norm} form
\[
  C = \begin{pmatrix}
    A & 0 & 0 & -b & 0 & -r_p \\
    0 & -A^T & -I_n & c & 0 & -r_d \\
    -c^T & b^T & 0 & 0 & -1 & -r_g \\
    r_d^T & r_p^T & 0 & r_g & 0 & 0
  \end{pmatrix}
  \in \R^{(m+n+2) \times (2n+m+3)}.
\]
The KKT system is
\begin{equation} \label{eq:kkt}
  \begin{pmatrix} G & C^T \\ C & 0 \end{pmatrix}
  \begin{pmatrix} z \\ \nu \end{pmatrix}
  =
  \begin{pmatrix} \text{rhs}_p \\ d_{\text{eq}} \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
where $\nu = (\nu_p, \nu_d, \nu_g, \nu_n) \in \R^{m+n+2}$ are the equality duals and
\[
  d_{\text{eq}} = (0, 0, 0, -\alpha)^T.
\]

\subsection{Right-hand side}

Since $b_{\text{cone}} = 0$ (all cone constraints have zero affine term), the RHS at barrier parameter $k$ simplifies to
\[
  (\text{rhs}_p)_i = \begin{cases}
    -k \cdot (\alpha) + 2w_i & \text{if } i = \theta \text{ (cost component)}, \\
    2w_i & \text{if } i \text{ is a cone variable}, \\
    0 & \text{if } i \text{ is a free variable } (y).
  \end{cases}
\]
More precisely, at the cone variable positions:
\begin{align}
  (\text{rhs}_p)_x &= 2 w_x, \\
  (\text{rhs}_p)_y &= 0, \\
  (\text{rhs}_p)_s &= 2 w_s, \\
  (\text{rhs}_p)_\tau &= 2 w_\tau, \\
  (\text{rhs}_p)_\kappa &= 2 w_\kappa, \\
  (\text{rhs}_p)_\theta &= -k \alpha.
\end{align}

\subsection{Direction from the KKT solution}

Let $z = (x^*, y^*, s^*, \tau^*, \kappa^*, \theta^*)$ be the KKT solution. The cone ``slack'' is
\[
  S_i = -z_i \quad \text{(since } b_{\text{cone}} = 0 \text{ and } \mathcal{A}_{\text{cone}} = I\text{)}.
\]
The direction components are
\begin{align}
  d_{x,i} &= 1 - w_{x,i} \cdot x^*_i, \label{eq:dx} \\
  d_{s,i} &= 1 - w_{s,i} \cdot s^*_i, \label{eq:ds} \\
  d_\tau &= 1 - w_\tau \cdot \tau^*, \\
  d_\kappa &= 1 - w_\kappa \cdot \kappa^*.
\end{align}

\section{Analysis: is $d_x = -d_s$?}

\subsection{At the fixed point}

At the fixed point $(\hat{x}, \hat{s}, \hat{y}, \hat{\tau}, \hat{\kappa}, \theta = 1)$ with $W = I$ and $k = 1$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\hat{x} = \hat{s} = e_n$, so $d_{x,i} = 1 - 1 \cdot \hat{x}_i^* = 1 - \hat{x}_i^*$ and $d_{s,i} = 1 - \hat{s}_i^*$.
  \item By the symmetry of the embedding, the cost $\alpha \theta$ and the constraint structure couple $x$ and $s$ through the dual equality \eqref{eq:dual}: $-A^T y - s + c\tau = r_d \theta$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{General case}

From \eqref{eq:dx}--\eqref{eq:ds}:
\[
  d_{x,i} + d_{s,i} = 2 - w_{x,i} x^*_i - w_{s,i} s^*_i.
\]
For $d_x = -d_s$ we would need $d_{x,i} + d_{s,i} = 0$, i.e.,
\[
  w_{x,i} x^*_i + w_{s,i} s^*_i = 2 \quad \forall i.
\]

This holds at the analytic center (where $w_{x,i} x^*_i = w_{s,i} s^*_i = 1$ by centrality). However, it does \textbf{not} hold in general. The KKT solution $(x^*, s^*)$ depends on the equality constraints, and there is no structural reason for $w_x \circ x^* + w_s \circ s^*$ to equal $2e$.

\subsection{When $W = I$}

At $W = I$ (initial scaling), the condition becomes $x^*_i + s^*_i = 2$. From the RHS:
\[
  (\text{rhs}_p)_x = 2e, \quad (\text{rhs}_p)_s = 2e.
\]
The Gram contributions are $G_x = I$, $G_s = I$. The KKT system gives
\[
  x^* + C_x^T \nu = 2e, \qquad s^* + C_s^T \nu = 2e
\]
where $C_x^T$ and $C_s^T$ are the columns of $C^T$ corresponding to $x$ and $s$. From the equality constraints:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $C_x$ has rows: $A$ (primal), $0$ (dual), $-c^T$ (gap), $r_d^T$ (norm).
  \item $C_s$ has rows: $0$ (primal), $-I$ (dual), $0$ (gap), $0$ (norm).
\end{itemize}
So $C_s^T \nu = -\nu_d$ and $C_x^T \nu = A^T \nu_p - c \nu_g + r_d \nu_n$.

Therefore:
\begin{align}
  x^* &= 2e - A^T \nu_p + c \nu_g - r_d \nu_n, \\
  s^* &= 2e + \nu_d.
\end{align}
And:
\[
  x^*_i + s^*_i = 4 - (A^T \nu_p)_i + c_i \nu_g - (r_d)_i \nu_n + (\nu_d)_i.
\]
This equals $2$ only if
\[
  (A^T \nu_p)_i - c_i \nu_g + (r_d)_i \nu_n - (\nu_d)_i = 2 \quad \forall i,
\]
which is \textbf{not} generically true. Therefore $d_x \neq -d_s$ in general.

\subsection{Conclusion}

$d_x = -d_s$ does not hold. The $x$ and $s$ directions are coupled through the equality constraints but are not negatives of each other. The rank of the product cone is $R = 2n + 2$, and all $2n + 2$ direction components are independently determined by the KKT system. Reducing the effective dimension requires exploiting the equality structure to eliminate variables from the KKT system, not a simple sign relationship.
